Welcome to Borrowly, a hyper-local peer-to-peer neighborhood sharing community (1–5 km radius). By accessing or using our mobile application and web services, you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms and Conditions.

1. Physical Cash & In-Person Handover Settlement

Borrowly operates on a peer-to-peer, in-person settlement model:

  • All rental fees, daily prices, and refundable security deposits are settled physically in-person between neighbors during item handover.
  • Borrowly processes zero digital in-app payment transactions and charges zero commission fees.
  • Item condition checks, handover verification, and deposit returns are handled directly between the item Owner and Borrower upon item return.

2. User Responsibilities & Item Care

As a member of the Borrowly community, you agree to:

  • Treat borrowed neighbor items with maximum care and return them on the agreed return date in the same condition as received.
  • Accurately describe items listed for loan, including any existing wear or usage instructions.
  • Provide honest communication during in-app neighbor chats and respect neighborhood boundaries (1–5 km radius).

3. Security Deposits & Handover Agreements

Item Owners may request a refundable cash security deposit during physical handover. Borrowers and Owners agree that security deposits are refunded in full upon return of the item in original condition.

4. Liability & Safety

Borrowly provides the platform to connect verified local neighbors. Members are encouraged to meet in safe, well-lit local areas for physical item handover.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding these Terms & Conditions, please contact us via our community support or GitHub repository.
