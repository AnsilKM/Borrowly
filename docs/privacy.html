Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 3, 2026

At Borrowly, protecting your privacy and securing your local neighborhood data is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when using the Borrowly app and services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile picture provided during Google Sign-In or account creation.
  • Hyper-Local Distance Radius: Coarse distance radius (1–5 km) used exclusively for displaying nearby item listings. Your exact GPS address is never publicly displayed.
  • Item Listings & Messages: Product details, photos, and real-time chat messages sent between neighbors to coordinate physical item handovers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Display nearby item listings within your selected distance radius (1–5 km).
  • Enable real-time messaging between item Owners and Borrowers.
  • Maintain account security and prevent fraudulent listings.

3. Zero Third-Party Data Selling

Borrowly does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data, location information, or messaging history with any third-party advertisers.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your session and user profile data are secured via Supabase PostgreSQL authentication with Row-Level Security (RLS) policies, alongside encrypted local Hive caching (< 5ms response time).

5. Contact Us

For any privacy inquiries or account data deletion requests, please reach out through our community repository or app settings.
