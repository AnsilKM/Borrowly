Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 6, 2026
Core Commitment: Borrowly is designed with privacy at its core. We only collect essential account details (Google OAuth) and approximate location data to show you nearby items from neighbors in your radius.

1. Information We Collect

To provide peer-to-peer neighborhood sharing, Borrowly collects:

  • Account & Profile Information: Name, email address, and profile photo fetched via Google Sign-In authentication.
  • Approximate Location Data: Device GPS coordinates (or neighborhood names) used strictly for executing PostGIS geospatial distance queries to display items within your selected search radius (e.g., 0.5 km to 15 km).
  • Item Listings & Messages: Photos, descriptions, daily rates, security deposit amounts, and real-time chat messages sent to item owners.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for the following purposes:

  • Exclusively calculating distance between neighbors for item borrowing requests.
  • Connecting borrowers with item owners via real-time messaging threads.
  • Maintaining platform security and preventing fraudulent account creation.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is securely transmitted over HTTPS and stored in our PostgreSQL backend hosted on Supabase with strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies. Local device caching utilizes encrypted Hive isolates for fast offline access.

4. Physical Settlement & Payments

Borrowly operates on a physical cash and in-person settlement model. Borrowly does not process, record, or store any digital payment gateway credentials, credit card numbers, or banking details.

5. Third-Party Services

We integrate with trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Google Authentication: For secure identity verification.
  • Supabase: For database hosting, authentication, and storage CDN.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests regarding your data privacy, please contact our support team at privacy@borrowly.com.
